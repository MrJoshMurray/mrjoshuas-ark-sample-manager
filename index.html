MrJoshua's ARK Sample Manager

Finds 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAVs in your sample library and sorts them into the eight ARK banks (WAV/1 to WAV/8). Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and your originals are never changed.

Files are sorted by looking for words like "kick", "snare", "hat" or "pad" in the file name and folder names, and by file size (short files are one-shots, long ones are loops and textures). The app can't hear the audio, so a few files may land in the wrong bank.

To keep the pack varied, it skips duplicate copies of the same file and files whose names differ only by a number (AHH_1, AHH_2, AHH_3 gives just one), and takes only a limited number from any one folder, so no single sample pack takes over a bank. It can't hear the audio, so it won't catch samples that merely sound alike under different names.
